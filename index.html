Interactive Lab Guide

What to Change: Modify velocity direction (av) or magnetic field (B). Set E = 0 to observe pure magnetic deflection, or set v = 0 to verify that magnetic force vanishes.

What to Observe: Notice how the Cyan Force vector (F) remains strictly perpendicular to both Velocity (v) and Magnetic Field (B) due to the cross-product rule.
